# Statistical Answer Review
Author: Shofi Ahmed Uddin

## Buyer problem
Find numerical errors, unsupported inferences and missing assumptions before an AI-generated analysis reaches a client.

## Pilot contract
- Buyer: AI teams, research consultancies and technical publishers.
- Scope: Up to 10 supplied statistical answers, one use context, and an agreed reference method.
- Outputs: A claim-by-claim findings table, reproducible numerical checks, and a concise correction memo.
- Inputs: supplied answers/data and reference documents; intended use; named accountable owner; agreed access route.
- Acceptance: every in-scope item is linked to evidence and a finding; every computed figure has a rerun route; missing inputs and unresolved judgments are explicit.
- Delivery: agree after a sample inspection. Scope or data changes require a revised agreement.
- Fee: fixed quotation after scoping; no automatic subscription.
- Exclusions: certification, legal opinion, production deployment, unrestricted security testing, guaranteed commercial outcomes.

## Evidence boundary
Reference calculations and semantic judgments are reported separately. The frozen benchmark remains unchanged; reviewer receipt recovery is still pending.
