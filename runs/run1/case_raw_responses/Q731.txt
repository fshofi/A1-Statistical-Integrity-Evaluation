The stated formula gives:

```text
nominal_lower: 10.040036
nominal_upper: 13.959964
```

However, the interval is not valid for this sampling design because the 36 measurements are perfectly correlated repeats from one production unit. The effective sample size is therefore 1, not 36.

Using the effective sample size:

```text
corrected_lower: 0.240216
corrected_upper: 23.759784
```

This uses \(12 \pm 1.959963984540054 \times 6\). The correction reflects the absence of independent replication across production units.
